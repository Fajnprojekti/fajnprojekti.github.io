Privacy policy

This policy describes the MMIOT Communications module and its optional Gmail connection. Each private installation is operated by its owner.

Data accessed and why

When you connect Gmail, MMIOT receives the selected account’s email address, Google account identifier, and verified-email status to confirm the account. It receives authorization tokens that allow the installation to send email on your behalf. MMIOT does not receive your Google password.

The requested Google permissions are OpenID account identification, email-address identification, and Gmail send-only access. MMIOT does not request mailbox-reading, searching, or message-deletion access.

For an authorized outgoing message, MMIOT processes the recipient’s email address, subject, and message text. Invitations and security notifications are prepared by the corresponding MMIOT feature. Provider identifiers, connection state, operation times, delivery state, and bounded error codes support administration and troubleshooting.

Use and sharing

Google account data is used to authenticate the selected connection and deliver the email requested by MMIOT features. Message data and necessary authorization data are transmitted to Google to perform those functions. Other configured providers receive the data required for their selected communication channel.

MMIOT Communications does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. Google user data is not shared with unrelated third parties.

MMIOT Communications’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and protection

The connected installation stores provider credentials encrypted with AES-256-GCM and a separate encryption key. Temporary enrollment material on a Windows setup computer is protected for the current Windows user. Google refresh tokens are not stored by the Android, Web, or Desktop application interfaces.

The installation’s operator controls its server, database, backups, and access. Delivery and administration records may remain in that database and its backups. Data sent to Google is also handled under Google’s own policies.

Retention, deletion, and revocation

Saved connection credentials remain available until the connection is deleted or replaced by the owner. Deleting a provider removes its active local credentials and disables its use. Administration and delivery records, and older backups, may remain under the installation owner’s retention arrangements.

MMIOT attempts to revoke a deleted Gmail connection when doing so will not invalidate another saved Gmail connection. The result is shown separately from local deletion. You can revoke MMIOT’s access directly at any time in your Google Account connections. Previously sent email cannot be recalled by deleting the connection.

To request deletion of installation records or backups, contact the owner of that installation. Keep passwords, tokens, and private message contents out of public support channels.

This public website

This static information site has no application login, forms, advertising, analytics scripts, or embedded third-party content. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact your MMIOT installation’s owner. For the Gmail connection, the application’s support email is shown on Google’s consent screen. Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated effective date.
