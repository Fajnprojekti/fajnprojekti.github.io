About MMIOT

MMIOT is a modular platform for connecting devices, data, and services. MMIOT Communications provides communication-provider management: adding, verifying, activating, disabling, and deleting connections.

Gmail connection

A Gmail account can be connected through Google’s consent screen. MMIOT requests permission to send email and the basic account identity needed to confirm the selected account. It does not request permission to read your inbox, search your mail, or delete messages.

Connecting or verifying an account does not send email. Sending becomes available only after the owner verifies and activates the connection in MMIOT.

Application information and support

This website provides public information about MMIOT Communications. It does not provide a mailbox, account enrollment, or access to MMIOT installations.

Application support is available through the support address shown on the Google consent screen. Installation-specific support and data requests are handled by the corresponding administrator.
