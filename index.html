Communication under the owner’s control.

MMIOT Communications connects a privately operated MMIOT installation to the communication provider chosen by its owner.

What the application does

The module manages email providers and compatible SMS relays for MMIOT invitations and security notifications. The owner can add a connection, verify it, activate it, disable its channel, or delete the connection.

Gmail connection

A Gmail account can be connected through Google’s consent screen. MMIOT requests permission to send email and the basic account identity needed to confirm the selected account. It does not request permission to read your inbox, search your mail, or delete messages.

Connecting or verifying an account does not send email. Sending becomes available only after the owner verifies and activates the connection in MMIOT.

Your installation, your settings

MMIOT runs on infrastructure controlled by its operator. This public information site does not provide a mailbox, account enrollment, or access to a private MMIOT installation.

For setup, support, and data requests, contact your MMIOT installation’s owner or use the support address shown on the Google consent screen.

Before connecting an account

Read how MMIOT uses and stores connection data in the privacy policy and review the terms of use.
