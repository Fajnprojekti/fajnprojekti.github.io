Terms of use

MMIOT Communications is a provider-management module for privately operated MMIOT installations.

Authorized use

Connect only accounts you control or are authorized to use. The installation owner is responsible for authorized recipients, message content, access permissions, and the configuration of each communication channel. Do not use the module for unsolicited bulk messaging, deception, or unlawful activity.

Provider services

Google and other selected communication providers operate independently and apply their own terms, limits, and availability. An email acceptance response does not guarantee delivery to the recipient’s inbox. A connection can require renewed authorization or become unavailable.

Operation and security

The owner controls the MMIOT installation, its updates, credentials, encryption-key backup, and retained records. Keep authentication factors private. Review provider state before relying on notifications. Do not rely on email or SMS as the sole means of emergency notification.

Disconnecting

The owner can disable a channel or delete a provider in MMIOT. Google access can also be revoked in the Google Account settings. Deletion does not recall messages already submitted to a provider. See the privacy policy for retention and revocation details.

Support and scope

This website provides application information; it does not enroll users into private installations or offer a paid service. Contact your installation’s owner for access, support, or data requests. The Gmail application’s support address is displayed on Google’s consent screen.
